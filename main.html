What is consciousness?

The current general definition of consciousness is the capacity of subjective experience and awareness of self, surrounding, thoughts, and sensation. For laypeople, it's obvious; for researchers, it's ambiguous, if not it's notoriouslly challenging to define. The main issue that makes the study of consciousness so challenging is that it's a first person experience that can't be accessed/viewed with our current science that depends on 3rd person view, and there is no agreed definition/target phenomenon of it (levels vs contents, wakefulness vs selfhood, access vs phenomenal consciousness). It basically lacks the consensus of how to measure consciousness empirically. In practice, that means different labs can be studying different things while using the same word.
